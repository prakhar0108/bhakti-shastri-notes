### The essence of bhakti: a committed relationship [04:00 – 10:00]

**Context.** The teacher explains that feelings matter, but the essence of _bhakti_ is a relationship with Kṛṣṇa, and that relationship shows itself as commitment `04:00 – 06:00`.

#### The gopīs' commitment [04:00 – 06:00]

- The _gopīs_ are glorified because they were so committed to Kṛṣṇa.
- How do we know they were committed? When Kṛṣṇa called with His flute in the night, they left everything to go and meet Him.
- Giving up something in order to get something more pleasant is easy; the _gopīs_' giving up was not of that kind.
- They were willing to go to hell if that would free Kṛṣṇa from a headache — at that moment, that is the love; it is the commitment `06:00`.

#### Feelings expressed through commitment [06:00]

- Feelings are important, but they must be expressed through commitment.
- The substance of _bhakti_ is practical activity — how we are serving Kṛṣṇa.

#### Selfless love in this world [06:00 – 08:00]

- The closest we come to deep selfless love in this world is in a mother's relationship with her baby.
- When an alarm clock rings at night, we shut it off because we want to keep sleeping; comparatively speaking, a baby is like an alarm clock — yet the mother responds.

#### The king and the two women [08:00]

- The authenticity of love is shown in a Bible story of a king: two women both claimed one baby girl as theirs.
- The king said that since both claimed the baby, it would be cut — a test through which authentic love is revealed.

<Callout type="idea" title="Takeaway">
Love for Kṛṣṇa is proved not by feelings alone but by commitment — like the gopīs who left everything at the sound of His flute and would accept hell to relieve His headache. [04:00 – 06:00]
</Callout>

### The substance of bhakti: practical activity [10:00 – 22:00]

**Context.** Having said that the substance of _bhakti_ is practical activity, the teacher illustrates it from Śrīla Prabhupāda's life and mission `10:00`.

#### Prabhupāda's practical mission [10:00 – 12:00]

- Prabhupāda wanted to spread the mission of Lord Caitanya and personally thought about how it would spread.
- He went to America and from there all over the world, circling the globe almost fourteen times, preaching so that Kṛṣṇa consciousness would spread.
- He engaged people in practical service — cooking and taking _prasāda_, serving, washing — even those who were not yet initiated or practising `12:00`.

#### Breaking the walls of misconception [14:00 – 16:00]

- Misconceptions are like walls closing our heads to Kṛṣṇa's beauty and glory; they stop us from understanding and loving Kṛṣṇa.
- Preaching removes the misconceptions that keep people from going to Kṛṣṇa.
- In modern times one's practice benefits not only oneself but others also `16:00`.

#### Everyone engaged in service [16:00]

- The important point is that everybody should be engaged in service, in the mood of being ready to do whatever activity is required.
- The facilities we provide, such as the big temples being built, are so that people come to the Lord.

#### The Vṛndāvana temple president's account [16:00 – 20:00]

- The teacher cites a Vyāsa-pūjā offering by a devotee who was temple president in Vṛndāvana when the Kṛṣṇa-Balarāma temple was started.
- He had just come to Vṛndāvana for the first time and was in a mood of thinking deeply about Kṛṣṇa all the way from Delhi.
- Throughout the car ride from Delhi to Vṛndāvana, Prabhupāda spoke about practical matters — what he wanted the devotee to do `18:00 – 20:00`.
- The teacher's point: Prabhupāda could very easily have been absorbed in thinking of Kṛṣṇa, but devotion is not just imagination or sentimentalism; Prabhupāda showed the advantage of practical devotional service `20:00`.

#### Seeing everything in terms of service [20:00 – 22:00]

- Some people in spirituality may be seekers of the transcendent without having any clear concepts.
- In devotional service, by contrast, things are seen in terms of their potential for Kṛṣṇa's service `22:00`.

<Callout type="idea" title="Takeaway">
Prabhupāda's devotion was intensely practical — travelling the world, engaging everyone in service, discussing practical matters even on the way to Vṛndāvana — showing that the substance of bhakti is service, not sentiment. [10:00 – 20:00]
</Callout>

### Service: the vehicle and the engine of devotion [22:00 – 36:00]

**Context.** The teacher analyses the phrase "devotional service": what each word contributes and how the two work together `22:00`.

**Key Terms Explained by the Teacher**

- _Service_ — the external component: what we do practically for Kṛṣṇa `22:00`.
- _Devotion_ — the emotional, internal component: the remembrance we cultivate within `22:00`.
- _Exhibition_ — for public consumption, a public show `24:00`.
- _Expression_ — for the object of devotion `24:00`.

#### Serve externally, remember internally [22:00]

- Broadly, devotional service has two components: service outside and devotional remembrance inside.
- While engaged in service, we try to remember Kṛṣṇa.

#### Exhibition versus expression [24:00 – 26:00]

- Devotion is not exhibition but expression.
- The mother example from earlier: a mother expresses her love not just by saying "I love you" to her child, but by practical service to the child; both are important.
- Similarly, service is the way we practically express devotion to the Lord.
- Otherwise anybody could claim "I am advanced, I love"; love is not just a feeling in the heart — the baby needs service `26:00`.

#### Service as the vehicle [28:00 – 30:00]

- If everyone in a place were simply lost in thought and nobody did anything, that would not be a place of active love.
- Love is expressed through service, and through that expression it is reciprocated in very deep and rich ways.
- A vehicle carries us somewhere: to go from here to Kolkata I need a train. Likewise, the devotion in the heart has to be expressed and conveyed to Kṛṣṇa through service `28:00 – 30:00`.

#### Service as the engine [30:00]

- What about when we are not feeling any emotion?
- Service is also the engine of devotion: rendering service stimulates devotional feelings.

#### Reviving a forgotten relationship [30:00 – 36:00]

- Souls have an original, eternal relationship with Kṛṣṇa which we have forgotten; we are suffering from amnesia.
- Hearing and chanting stimulate and activate our covered memory `32:00`.
- The movie example: a hero who has lost his memory recovers it when stimuli connected with his past are provided `32:00`.
- Similarly, layers and layers of forgetfulness cover our attraction to Kṛṣṇa; exposure to devotional stimuli — the holy name, association, chanting, serving Kṛṣṇa — brings the memory back step by step `32:00 – 34:00`.
- We do not have to develop a new relationship: like the son of a millionaire who does not have to learn to love his father, we simply remember an earlier relationship. We should not think we have to learn to love Kṛṣṇa `34:00`.

<Callout type="idea" title="Takeaway">
Service is both the vehicle that carries our devotion to Kṛṣṇa and the engine that stirs devotion when feelings are absent — reviving, rather than creating, our eternal relationship with Him. [28:00 – 34:00]
</Callout>

### Cultivation: more than stopping nonsense [36:00 – 40:00]

**Context.** Returning to the word "cultivation," the teacher explains what it adds and why this background is needed before the six principles `36:00`.

#### Positive engagement [36:00]

- Cultivation is often understood as stopping nonsense activities.
- Devotional service does not only stop nonsense activities; it increases meaningful devotional activities.
- As discussed in an earlier class, if we engage in positive activities in Kṛṣṇa's service, the negative ones automatically go away.

#### Why the background matters [38:00]

- With a clear understanding of devotional cultivation, free from preconceptions, it will make sense how enthusiasm, patience and faith enable spiritual advancement.
- The discussion so far sets up the background for explaining the principles.

<Callout type="idea" title="Takeaway">
Bhakti is cultivation that crowds out the negative by multiplying positive service — the foundation on which enthusiasm, patience and faith can work. [36:00 – 38:00]
</Callout>

### Enthusiasm: endeavour with intelligence [40:00 – 48:00]

**Context.** The first of the favourable principles is enthusiasm, which Prabhupāda explains as endeavour with intelligence in Kṛṣṇa consciousness `40:00 – 42:00`.

#### Intelligence directed to Kṛṣṇa [42:00]

- Endeavour and intelligence are present in everyone, but they are not always invested in Kṛṣṇa consciousness.
- Here they are used for Kṛṣṇa's pleasure.
- Using intelligence this way means arranging for the vibration of the holy name, a picture of Kṛṣṇa and a devotional atmosphere around us, so that we naturally remember Kṛṣṇa.

#### The road to Mumbai [44:00]

- Just energy is not enough.
- If I am going to Mumbai and know the way, I will go enthusiastically and fast.
- But if I am going in the wrong direction, I should stop and make a U-turn.
- Enthusiasm is not only strength; it must come with intelligence — we have to know the right path and the wrong path.

#### Expanding the scope of service [44:00 – 48:00]

- Prabhupāda makes an interesting point about activities: everything and everyone is connected with Kṛṣṇa, because Kṛṣṇa is the source of everything `46:00`.
- Therefore everything can be used in Kṛṣṇa's service — for example, internet technology.
- In this way we expand our conception of devotion to include activities we would not normally consider devotional.
- Enthusiasm is therefore shown not only in devotional activities but also in the creativity and intelligence by which we expand the scope of service, thinking of more and more activities in Kṛṣṇa's service; this is an important parameter of enthusiasm `46:00 – 48:00`.

<Callout type="idea" title="Takeaway">
Enthusiasm is energy guided by intelligence for Kṛṣṇa's pleasure: it chooses the right direction and creatively finds ever more ways to use everything in His service. [40:00 – 48:00]
</Callout>
