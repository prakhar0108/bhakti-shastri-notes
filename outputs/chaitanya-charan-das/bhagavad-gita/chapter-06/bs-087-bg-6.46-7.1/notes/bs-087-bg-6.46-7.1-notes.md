## Shloka-Wise Notes & Analytical Commentary

### Bhagavad-gita 6.46 [00:00 – 10:00]

<!-- vedabase:6.46 -->

**Context.** The yogī is better than the ascetic, the empiricist and the fruitive worker; therefore Arjuna should be a yogī `00:00`.

**Key Terms Explained by the Teacher**

- **Yoga** — here primarily connection with the Absolute Truth, by whichever form of yoga `00:00 – 02:00`.
- _Jñānī_ — one who cultivates empiric knowledge, which often leads to the conception "I am God" `06:00 – 08:00`.
- _Karmī_ — one engaged in fruitive work `00:00`.
- _Śrama_ — labour; work is done for some fruit, and labour without fruit is useless — hence "a waste of time" `08:00`.

#### Connection is what matters [00:00 – 02:00]

- Having a connection with the Absolute Truth is more important than just doing austerities, just cultivating knowledge or just doing work — things that others may consider spiritual.
- Prabhupāda's purport: when we speak of yoga, we mean linking our consciousness with the Supreme Absolute Truth, possible by various processes `02:00`.

#### Asceticism without self-knowledge [02:00 – 06:00]

- Asceticism without self-knowledge is imperfect; self-knowledge means knowing the _ātmā_ not just as such, but as a part of the Supreme `02:00`.
- Prabhupāda: without remembrance of Kṛṣṇa, fasting is simply an exercise for fat removal `02:00`.
- Why people fast today `04:00 – 06:00`:
  - **Health** — "fasting retreats" under doctors, derived from nature therapy: when the body is not digesting new food, its energy is free for cleansing and repairing itself; some otherwise incurable diseases have been cured this way.
  - **Self-control** — to prove "I can control myself."
  - **Weight loss** — the most popular reason, in a society where fat people are almost treated as untouchables, while advertisements constantly tempt people with fast food that is also fat food — a catch-22.
- None of these is spiritual. Devotees may fast for health and use yoga for health, as Prabhupāda recommended fasting on _Ekādaśī_ and holy days — but they don't get caught up in that alone or divorce it from its spiritual purpose `06:00`.

#### Empiric knowledge and fruitive work [06:00 – 08:00]

- Empiric knowledge without surrender to the Supreme Lord is also imperfect; Prabhupāda is mildly critical — not calling it useless, but imperfect.
- His stronger critique is of fruitive activity: mere labour, a waste of time `08:00`.

#### Quoting out of context [08:00 – 10:00]

- Yogīs may be impersonalists or personalists; is Kṛṣṇa giving a blanket endorsement of yoga? No — what He means by yoga becomes clear only in the next verse.
- Many yoga teachers, to prove the Gītā glorifies yoga, quote 6.46 but not 6.47 — an example of quoting out of context.
- Kṛṣṇa does recommend yoga, but not just as the _aṣṭāṅga_ _sādhana_; He means yoga as connection — with the personal Absolute Truth `10:00`.

<Callout type="idea" title="Takeaway">
6.46 praises yoga as connection with the Absolute Truth over mere austerity, knowledge or work — fasting without remembering Kṛṣṇa is just fat removal — but what kind of yoga Kṛṣṇa means is revealed only in the next verse. [00:00 – 10:00]
</Callout>

### Bhagavad-gita 6.47 [10:00 – 38:00]

<!-- vedabase:6.47 -->

**Context.** Of all yogīs, the one who abides in Kṛṣṇa with faith, thinks of Him and worships Him is the most intimately united with Him and the highest. The teacher calls it one of the most often quoted verses of the Gītā by Śrīla Prabhupāda, especially when asked about yoga `10:00`.

**Key Terms Explained by the Teacher**

- _Yoginām_ — among all kinds of yogīs `10:00`, `16:00`.
- _Yukta_ — well-connected, well-executed, well-situated; the verse uses its superlative — "best among the well-connected" `12:00 – 14:00`.
- **Living in Kṛṣṇa** — to live in Him or in harmony with Him, by thinking of Him within oneself `16:00`.
- **Faith** — the conviction that the Lord in the heart is the ultimate truth and the object of service, worship, surrender and devotion `16:00 – 18:00`.
- _Bhajate_ — Prabhupāda focuses on this word: it does not just mean worship but service; we can worship many, but we serve the Absolute Truth `18:00`.

#### Good, better, best [10:00 – 14:00]

- Endorsements come in degrees — good, better, best; Sanskrit has similar comparative and superlative forms.
- In this chapter Kṛṣṇa calls a person _yukta_ in 6.8 and 6.18 `12:00`:
  - 6.8 — one satisfied by knowledge and realisation, situated high above material things, who has conquered the senses.
  - 6.18 — again one who is well situated.
- Both uses are essentially negative — about **disconnection** from matter. That is good: the person will not get more entangled `14:00`.
- But here Kṛṣṇa speaks of **connection** with Him — and that is best `14:00 – 16:00`.

#### Levels of faith in the Lord in the heart [16:00 – 18:00]

- Kṛṣṇa is in everyone's heart, but understanding differs:
  - **Faithless** — "I don't know whether Kṛṣṇa exists in the heart."
  - **Transitional** — "Kṛṣṇa in the heart is just a conception; ultimately one must go beyond." The teacher says _māyāvādīs_ are therefore also atheistic: atheists reject God by saying He doesn't exist, while they reject Him by calling Him a transitional reality — calling themselves "transtheistic."
  - **Faithful** — the Lord in the heart is the ultimate truth, the object of service and devotion; there is no going beyond.

#### Falling away from our position [18:00 – 20:00]

- Prabhupāda connects _bhajate_ with the word for falling away, used in this chapter for the deviated yogī.
- We are intrinsically connected to the Supreme Lord and meant forever for His love and service; if we don't love and serve Him, we fall from our spiritual position back into the material world of temptations.

#### The multi-storey building [20:00 – 24:00]

- Ultimately all yogas are meant to culminate in _bhakti-yoga_; here Prabhupāda uses "yoga" in the sense of the process.
- Imagine a building with floors above the ground, and staircases that each reach only up to a certain floor `20:00 – 22:00`:
  - First floor — _svarga_, reached by the staircase of _karma-kāṇḍa_.
  - Second floor — Brahman, reached by the staircase of _jñāna_.
  - Third floor — Paramātmā, reached by the staircase of yoga.
  - Fourth floor — Bhagavān, the topmost fulfilment.
- Two ways to see the other yogas `22:00 – 24:00`:
  - As **independent paths** to particular destinations — though, as later chapters (especially the ninth) show, they too need an element of _bhakti_ to succeed.
  - As **assistant paths**: the floors are connected, so one who reaches a lower floor is not trapped; he can find the staircase to the top if he eventually takes to _bhakti_.
- If one wants the fourth floor, it is best to take the staircase that goes directly there — not to climb to the first floor, look around and come back down `24:00`.

#### Why bhakti-yoga is the most complete [24:00 – 26:00]

- It includes **action** (the positive aspect of _karma-yoga_), **introspection** (contemplation to understand the Absolute) and **meditation** (directed thinking toward the Absolute) — the limbs of the other yogas.
- Beyond that, all our faculties follow the **heart**: when we love someone, we physically serve them, think of them and connect with them intellectually.
- Even head-driven intellectuals have a heart, and when their emotions are stirred, those emotions take control. Material emotions taking control is undesirable; spiritual emotions taking control is supremely desirable `26:00`.
- _Bhakti-yoga_ activates the heart toward Kṛṣṇa, and so it is topmost.

#### Śyāmasundara, faith and the guru [26:00 – 30:00]

- In this purport, for the first time in the Gītā, Prabhupāda describes Kṛṣṇa as all-beautiful: the ideal yogī concentrates on Kṛṣṇa, called Śyāmasundara, with a description from the _Brahma-saṁhitā_ `26:00 – 28:00`.
- Prabhupāda proceeds systematically — first the meaning of _bhajate_, then who is the most intimately united, then faith (_śraddhā_) `28:00`.
- He quotes a famous verse: to those who have implicit faith in the Lord and similarly in the spiritual master, everything is revealed. Interestingly, those who have such faith — even while still seekers — are called _mahātmā_ there `28:00`.
- He also quotes a definition of _bhakti_ from the _Gopāla-tāpanī Upaniṣad_, an important Upaniṣad about the worship of Gopāla frequently quoted by the _ācāryas_: serving the Supreme Lord without desires for gain in this life or the next — that is perfection `30:00`.

#### The first emphatic "me" [32:00 – 36:00]

- This is the first time Kṛṣṇa has said, in such a categorical, emphatic way, that connecting with Him is the highest `32:00`.
- Earlier references to Him, as the teacher traces them `32:00 – 34:00`:
  - 2.60s — fix the mind on Him in order to control the senses (instrumental, not yet as the ultimate goal).
  - 3.30 — offer the fruits of work to Him.
  - Fourth chapter — faith in Him and His words; understanding His appearance and activities brings liberation; 4.34 – 4.35: surrender to the guru, and then one sees all living beings in Him.
  - 5.29 — the only reference to "me" in the fifth chapter: He is the ultimate enjoyer of everything.
  - 6.14 – 6.15 — discussed earlier.
- But never as emphatically as here, at the end of the chapter `34:00`.

#### From renunciation in action to devotion in action [34:00 – 38:00]

- Arjuna's dialectic was action or renunciation. Kṛṣṇa revealed something more subtle — renunciation in action, integrating the two — and that has been the overall tenor of the first six chapters.
- From now on it will be **devotion in action**: not only "I am not connected with the world," but "I want to be connected with Kṛṣṇa" `36:00`.
- In the _bhakti_ section from 7.1, spiritual advancement is judged not by how much one is disconnected from the world but by how much one is connected with Kṛṣṇa `36:00`.
- The first six chapters set the scene; chapters 7 to 12 are the heart of the Gītā, on _bhakti_ `36:00`.
- The moving spotlight `38:00`:
  - First chapter — on the body.
  - From the second chapter — on the soul, liberated by sublime action (_karma-yoga_).
  - Now — from the _ātmā_ to the Paramātmā, revealed to be Kṛṣṇa Himself.

<Callout type="idea" title="Takeaway">
Disconnection from matter is good, but connection with Kṛṣṇa is best: the topmost yogī abides in Him with faith and serves Him, because bhakti engages action, introspection, meditation and — above all — the heart, the staircase that goes straight to the top floor. [12:00 – 26:00]
</Callout>

### Bhagavad-gita 7.1 [38:00 – 58:00]

<!-- vedabase:7.1 -->

**Context.** Kṛṣṇa tells Arjuna to hear how, practising yoga in full consciousness of Him with the mind attached to Him and taking shelter of Him, he can know Kṛṣṇa in full, free from doubt `42:00`.

**Key Terms Explained by the Teacher**

- **Hearing** — both the means by which Kṛṣṇa tells the path and the path itself `42:00 – 44:00`.
- **Free from doubt** — doubts, from basic to complex, are removed as knowledge increases `46:00 – 48:00`.
- **Knowing in full** — fully according to our capacity, not in totality `48:00`.
- **Shelter** — not an abstract conception but a practical action: the practice of devotional service `46:00`.

#### Overview of the seventh chapter [38:00 – 42:00]

- The chapter has 30 verses in three sections `38:00 – 40:00`:
  1. **Knowing Kṛṣṇa in full (1 – 12)**
     - 1 – 3: the glory of knowledge about Kṛṣṇa.
     - 4 – 7: Kṛṣṇa is the source of everything material and spiritual.
     - 8 – 12: Kṛṣṇa not only exists but pervades everything.
  2. **Surrender to Kṛṣṇa (13 – 25)**
     - 13 – 14: why surrender.
     - 15: those who don't surrender.
     - 16: those who do surrender.
     - 20 – 25: those who surrender to something other than Kṛṣṇa.
  3. **Liberation by knowledge of Kṛṣṇa (26 – 30)** — no further subdivisions.
- Flow: knowledge about Kṛṣṇa → that knowledge must be transformational, and the transformation is surrender → the transformation culminates in liberation `40:00`.
- The chapter is called knowledge of the Absolute; the tenth chapter will focus on Kṛṣṇa's _vibhūtis_, while this chapter gives general knowledge about Him `40:00`.
- Link with the sixth chapter: there Kṛṣṇa spoke of the yogī who always lives in Him and is liberated (6.31); now He offers an alternative process of liberation, based on knowledge of Him, without the laborious _aṣṭāṅga-yoga_ of going to the forest and renouncing everything — described further in the ninth and twelfth chapters `40:00 – 42:00`.

#### Hearing shows the path and is the path [42:00 – 44:00]

- Hearing tells us how to make spiritual advancement and move closer to Kṛṣṇa.
- Hearing is also the path, because hearing itself increases our attraction to Kṛṣṇa; everything begins with hearing.
- It is not the only path — there are nine limbs of devotional service — but hearing is a very important limb `44:00`.

#### Reading the verse bottom-up [44:00 – 48:00]

- 6.47 said the topmost yogī always thinks of Kṛṣṇa; but we cannot think of something constantly unless we have affection for it. So Kṛṣṇa now tells how to become attached to Him `44:00`.
- The process up to the end of the sixth chapter — _karma-yoga_, _aṣṭāṅga-yoga_, detachment, then attachment to Kṛṣṇa — is like a staircase; the process Kṛṣṇa now outlines is like an elevator to the same goal `44:00 – 46:00`.
- The stages, read from the bottom of the verse up `46:00 – 48:00`:
  1. **Hearing** regularly about Kṛṣṇa — His pastimes and philosophy.
  2. **Knowing** Kṛṣṇa properly, in full according to our capacity.
  3. **Freedom from doubt** — doubts are clarified and removed.
  4. **Taking shelter** — understanding Kṛṣṇa as our greatest well-wisher and the ultimate object of love.
  5. **Practising yoga** — devotional service.
  6. **Attachment of the mind** to Kṛṣṇa — the perfection.

#### Knowing "in full" and the doubts removed [48:00 – 50:00]

- No one can know Kṛṣṇa completely; in the tenth chapter it is said that only He knows Himself — and even He does not know Himself fully, because His energies keep expanding `48:00`.
- So we know Him fully according to our capacity, and are purified by that knowledge.
- Doubts range from basic ("Does God exist? Does God care for me?") to complex ("Is the holy name powerful enough to give ultimate liberation? Is it the only way in Kali-yuga? Is Kṛṣṇa the highest aspect of the Absolute Truth?") `48:00`.
- The _ācāryas_ have written so many books, and many devotees have studied them; that scholarship is available if we seek it `50:00`.

#### Not entertainment but attainment [50:00]

- We hear about Kṛṣṇa not for entertainment but for attainment — attainment of shelter in Kṛṣṇa.
- That shelter makes the mind attached to Kṛṣṇa, the perfection of spiritual life: an attached mind is not distracted by anything else, and we attain not just peacefulness but joyfulness, since Kṛṣṇa is supremely joyful.
- Prabhupāda's purport links this verse to the last verse of the sixth chapter: fixing the mind on Kṛṣṇa is possible by prescribed devotional service in nine forms, of which hearing is the most important `50:00`.

#### How hearing purifies the heart (Bhāgavatam 1.2.17 – 21) [52:00 – 58:00]

- The teacher calls 1.2.6 – 1.2.21 the most quoted section of the _Bhāgavatam_ in Prabhupāda's teachings `52:00`.
- The sequence as he explains it:
  - **Hearing purifies in two ways** — the sound vibration itself purifies, and our submissive reception of it activates Kṛṣṇa's presence in the heart, who then cleanses our impurities from within `52:00 – 54:00`.
  - **The Lord as well-wisher** — He wishes the welfare of all, but especially of devotees: a doctor treats every patient, yet his therapy works better on a cooperative patient than on one who is hostile and kicking. Kṛṣṇa is present in every heart whether we glorify or defy Him, but He reciprocates with those who want His help `52:00 – 54:00`.
  - **Serving the bhāgavata** — both the book _Bhāgavata_ and the person _bhāgavata_; through both we hear about Kṛṣṇa, and _bhakti_ awakens `54:00`.
  - **Freedom from the lower modes** — first we become detached from the negative and attached to the positive; when the pulls of the lower modes stop, we are situated in goodness and can move forward steadily `54:00 – 56:00`.
  - **Joyful service** — resentful devotional service does not lead to much advancement; joyful devotional service does. With a pleasant mind we serve, and scientific knowledge of Kṛṣṇa awakens in the heart `56:00`.
  - **Liberation from contamination** — then one becomes free from all contact with matter; attachments fall away, doubts go away, and one gets transcendental vision and becomes attached to Kṛṣṇa `58:00`.
- Prabhupāda uses this sequence to explain the words for hearing and for freedom from doubt in 7.1 `58:00`.

<Callout type="idea" title="Takeaway">
Hearing both shows the path and is the path: by hearing about Kṛṣṇa — for attainment, not entertainment — we come to know Him according to our capacity, are freed from doubt, take shelter of Him and find the mind naturally attached to Him. [42:00 – 58:00]
</Callout>
