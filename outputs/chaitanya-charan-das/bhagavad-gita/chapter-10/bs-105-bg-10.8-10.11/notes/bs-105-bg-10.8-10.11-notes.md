## Continuity & Recap from Preceding Class

- The Gītā has risen from a question on the battlefield to profound philosophical topics whose height is rarely seen before or after; by the eighteenth chapter it comes down to "I will fight," because the action called for on the battlefield is in line with the pinnacle of existence `00:00 – 02:00`.
- The ninth chapter is called the most confidential knowledge; this chapter too begins with Kṛṣṇa saying He will give the supreme word `02:00`.
- Kṛṣṇa has been building up: in 10.3 this knowledge frees one from reactions; in 10.7 it makes one wholeheartedly devoted — fixed in Him without doubt and without alternatives, as discussed in the last class. In these four verses He describes how this happens `02:00`.
- Immediately after these verses comes the only place in the Gītā where Arjuna paraphrases his understanding of Kṛṣṇa's position. In the eleventh chapter Arjuna also offers prayers describing what he sees in the universal form, but that is not exactly a paraphrase of what Kṛṣṇa has spoken `02:00 – 04:00`.

### The four verses as one unit [04:00 – 08:00]

- **10.8** gives the summary; **10.9–10.11** elaborate it `04:00`.
- 10.8: knowing that Kṛṣṇa is the source of everything, one becomes enlightened and devoted.
- 10.10: Kṛṣṇa gives intelligence (_buddhi_); 10.11: present in the heart, He removes the darkness with the torchlight of knowledge.
- **Head and heart:** the verses describe the redirection of the heart (devotion — elaborated in 10.9) and the redirection of the head (illumination — elaborated in 10.10–10.11) `04:00 – 06:00`.
- The teacher reads the four verses through _sambandha_, _abhidheya_ and _prayojana_ `06:00`:
  - _Sambandha_ — understanding our relationship with the ultimate reality, which is personal: Kṛṣṇa.
  - _Abhidheya_ — the process by which that relationship is developed: _bhakti_.
  - _Prayojana_ — the result: _prema_.
- **The car advertisement** `06:00 – 08:00`:
  - _Sambandha_: seeing an advertisement, "I want that car" — a relationship of possession.
  - _Abhidheya_: "I don't have the money; I'll take a car loan."
  - _Prayojana_: driving the car proudly, waiting to see the neighbours' gaze — and then finding the neighbour coming in his own car.
  - In material life, just when we expect the fruit of happiness, it doesn't come, or it comes so little and goes.
- In any activity there is a relationship, a process and a result; in these four verses the whole philosophy is compressed in a concise way `08:00`.

## Shloka-Wise Notes & Analytical Commentary

### Bhagavad-gita 10.8 [08:00 – 34:00]

<!-- vedabase:10.8 -->

**Context.** Kṛṣṇa declares that He is the source of all, that everything emanates from Him, and that the wise who know this engage in His devotional service with all their hearts `10:00 – 14:00`.

**Key Terms Explained by the Teacher**

- _Source and maintainer_ — not only is everything from Kṛṣṇa; everything stands by Him, is arranged by Him, and its whole maintenance goes on by His arrangement `10:00`.
- _Budha_ — the wise, enlightened person; Prabhupāda says such a person rejects unauthorised commentators and interpretations `28:00`.
- _Bhāva-samanvita_ — with the whole heart: knowledge of Kṛṣṇa's position makes the person enlightened and also attracted to offer the whole heart `14:00`.

#### Not a distant designer, but a proximate overseer [10:00 – 12:00]

- Some people's idea of God is a distant designer; the Gītā very directly substantiates that God is also a proximate, personal overseer of everything.
- He is present as _Paramātmā_ in the heart of everyone.
- He is also neutral (_udāsīna_) towards those who do not want Him to intervene; but for those who turn towards Him, He is personally there `12:00`.

#### Head and heart both transformed [14:00]

- Knowing Kṛṣṇa's position enlightens the head; being attracted to offer the whole heart transforms the heart — both are directed towards Kṛṣṇa.
- Prabhupāda gives a "galaxy of supports" from various scriptures to substantiate that Kṛṣṇa is the source of everything and the Absolute Truth `14:00`:
  - The _Gopāla-tāpanī Upaniṣad_: Kṛṣṇa instructed Brahmā in Vedic knowledge in the past `14:00 – 16:00`.
  - The _Nārāyaṇa Upaniṣad_: everyone has come from Nārāyaṇa `16:00 – 18:00`.
- Some people think Brahmā is the source; these quotes show the ultimate source is Kṛṣṇa `16:00`.

#### How creation descends — and varies [18:00 – 20:00]

- The _Bhāgavatam_ sometimes describes different processes for the origin of various living beings; the details of creation need not be identical every time — they can vary across universes and time cycles.
- **Emigrant ancestor:** if a person emigrated from India to some area long ago, we may say of a king, a warrior and a businessman there, "he came from him" — not all are his direct children, but all descend from him.
- Similarly people come from Nārāyaṇa ultimately, not necessarily directly — though even that can happen if the Lord so wills in some universe — and Nārāyaṇa also comes from Kṛṣṇa `20:00`.

#### "The Lord desired to create living entities" [20:00 – 22:00]

- Since souls are eternal, this purport line does not refer to the spiritual level, where souls are never created.
- It refers to the beginning of the universe: at the end of Brahmā's life the universe is destroyed and the universal substratum enters Viṣṇu; when the Lord desires again, the universe emanates from His body, He glances, and creation is triggered.
- Souls are not being created but becoming embodied: souls who already wanted to come to the material world are given bodies. The Lord's desire is to restart creation, allowing souls to play out their desires and ultimately come back to Him `22:00`.

#### Degrees of omniscience [24:00 – 26:00]

- People do not know this because they are deluded by _māyā_.
- English vocabulary is not enough for the Vedic understanding of existence: "omni" means "all," but all of what — all of existence, or all of one universe?
- Demigods such as Yamarāja are omniscient in a limited sense, within a particular universe; only Lord Viṣṇu, the Supersoul who is everywhere, is completely omniscient.
- The demigods' partial omniscience matches their portfolio in universal management: to execute the responsibility entrusted to them, they are given the power and manifest abilities most people don't have `26:00`.

#### "Everything" means everything [26:00 – 28:00]

- Similar to Kṛṣṇa's statement in 9.10 that nature works under His direction, here He declares that everything comes from Him — no exceptions.
- From Kṛṣṇa comes Balarāma; from Him further expansions, including Nārāyaṇa; then Mahā-Viṣṇu; then the Viṣṇu forms within the universe — and so the whole universe goes on. From the cosmological point of view, the Lord is the source of everyone `26:00 – 28:00`.

#### Self-evident, yet a commentary? [28:00 – 34:00]

- Prabhupāda says these verses make their meaning self-evident; there is no need for any interpretation `28:00`.
- **Mahāprabhu and Sārvabhauma Bhaṭṭācārya** — "an iron fist in a velvet glove" `28:00 – 30:00`: hearing Sārvabhauma's explanation, Mahāprabhu said in effect, "I understand the sūtras, but I don't understand what you are saying." Like a student telling a teacher this, it implies:
  1. "I understand the text without you" — the teacher is unnecessary;
  2. "Your explanation is like a cloud covering the sun";
  3. "What you are explaining is a misunderstanding."
- But if no interpretation is needed, why does Prabhupāda give a commentary? `30:00`
- "Self-evident" may not be evident to us: the sun gives light, but clouds over our heads may block it. The Gītā's meaning is self-evident, but for us it is covered by our preconceptions, prejudices, misconceptions and misdirections `32:00`.
- To explain the Gītā one must love Kṛṣṇa, because that is its message; not loving Kṛṣṇa, one is not living the Gītā and so not understanding it — it is not a theoretical but a transformational subject `32:00`.
- Other interpretations may contain some valuable things, but the real understanding is Kṛṣṇa `34:00`.
- In _sambandha_ terms, 10.8 gives the knowledge that Kṛṣṇa is the source of everything; its last part points to the result — love for Kṛṣṇa, devotion `34:00`.

<Callout type="idea" title="Takeaway">
Kṛṣṇa is not a distant designer but the source and maintainer of everything, personally present in the heart; knowing this enlightens the head and wins the heart — and since the Gītā is a transformational subject, its self-evident meaning is uncovered only by those who love Kṛṣṇa. [10:00 – 34:00]
</Callout>

### Bhagavad-gita 10.9 [34:00 – 44:00]

<!-- vedabase:10.9 -->

**Context.** Kṛṣṇa describes His pure devotees: their thoughts and lives are absorbed in Him, they enlighten one another and converse about Him, and they derive great satisfaction and bliss `34:00 – 42:00`.

**Key Terms Explained by the Teacher**

- _Tuṣyanti_ — devotionally satisfied: "Whatever I am getting, I'm satisfied" `40:00`.
- _Ramanti_ — the word used, for example, in _Rādhā-ramaṇa_, in the context of _mādhurya_: looking forward with delight — "so much more ecstasy waiting for me in the future" `40:00`.

#### The mind's home [36:00]

- Everyone has some "home" in the mind; when nothing is demanding our attention — no emergency — the mind gravitates there.
- A devotee may think about other things — whatever service is required, whatever practical needs there are — but Kṛṣṇa is the home: as soon as there is nothing else to do, the thoughts come back to Him.
- _Bhakti_ means changing our inner home; we advance spiritually to the extent our inner home changes from worldly things to Kṛṣṇa.

#### The traveller returning home [36:00 – 38:00]

- The _Bhāgavatam_ also speaks of this: like a traveller who, after a long and troublesome journey, reaches home and finds security and happiness, and does not want to leave again —
- similarly, a purified soul never gives up Kṛṣṇa's lotus feet, and is freed from all miseries.
- When we bring our consciousness to Kṛṣṇa and remember Him, we feel truly at home `38:00`.

#### Relishing Kṛṣṇa-kathā together [40:00]

- Devotees' body, mind and speech are all used in Kṛṣṇa's service.
- Discussing Kṛṣṇa together is demonstrated by Śrī Caitanya Mahāprabhu in His final pastimes at Purī, in the Gambhīrā, with His intimate associates.
- The _Bhāgavatam_ also says the joy of _kṛṣṇa-kathā_ is the greatest joy — a heartfelt delight `40:00 – 42:00`.

#### The creeper of devotion [42:00 – 44:00]

- From the _Caitanya-caritāmṛta_ (Madhya-līlā, chapter 19), the instruction on the creeper of devotion:
  - The seed is protected and watered — the water is _śravaṇa_ and _kīrtana_.
  - It grows into a beautiful creeper and gives flowers and fruits.
  - It grows beyond the coverings of the universe, through Brahmaloka, up to the spiritual sky, and within the spiritual sky to Goloka, where it reaches Kṛṣṇa's lotus feet.
- This is advancement in the relationship with Kṛṣṇa.
- 10.9 speaks primarily of _abhidheya_ — how to practise devotional service — while also touching on _prayojana_ `44:00`.

<Callout type="idea" title="Takeaway">
Bhakti changes the mind's inner "home" from worldly things to Kṛṣṇa; devotees absorbed in Him find satisfaction in what they have and delight in what awaits, especially in discussing Kṛṣṇa together — like a traveller who has finally come home. [36:00 – 42:00]
</Callout>

### Bhagavad-gita 10.10 [44:00 – 56:00]

<!-- vedabase:10.10 -->

**Context.** To those constantly devoted and serving Him with love, Kṛṣṇa gives the understanding by which they can come to Him `44:00 – 48:00`.

**Key Terms Explained by the Teacher**

- _With love_ — not grudgingly: if we serve with affection, _bhakti_ brings illumination; if with resentment, we prevent Kṛṣṇa from giving what He wants to give `46:00`.
- _Buddhi-yoga_ — both the _yoga_ that grants _buddhi_, and the _buddhi_ that helps one do _yoga_ — _yoga_ meaning connection with the Absolute Truth, Kṛṣṇa `50:00`.

#### "Just chanting and dancing?" [46:00]

- A common question: "You only do _bhakti_ — chanting and dancing. You should be sitting and discussing analytically. Why dance on the streets like sentimentalists?"
- The teacher recalls the humble statement "My spiritual master said I was not very intelligent, so I should just chant" — and people do think devotees lack intelligence.
- Kṛṣṇa assures here that He Himself gives that intelligence. He is not demanding that devotees be very intelligent first; He asks for service with love.

#### Knowledge that leads to Him [48:00]

- In context, Kṛṣṇa says: "I am the Supreme Truth, and I give the knowledge by which you will come back to Me" — not knowledge by which something else will happen.
- This is a clearly and consistently devotional set of verses, which causes many headaches for _māyāvādīs_ trying to give an impersonal interpretation.

#### Kṛṣṇa provides what is required [50:00 – 54:00]

- When you have _bhakti_, _buddhi_ comes: "If you are deficient in knowledge, it doesn't matter; I will provide it."
- The teacher cites a _Bhāgavatam_ verse: through _bhakti-yoga_, the science of God dawns for one freed from material association `52:00`.
- Engage in devotional service, and while engaged, Kṛṣṇa provides what is required — you don't have to do anything separately.
- At the fundamental level we need to know the goal; scripture gives the path. As we walk it, Kṛṣṇa guides from within, the path becomes clearer, and we keep moving forward and ultimately come to Him without difficulty `54:00`.

#### Submit the intellect, don't scrutinise Kṛṣṇa [54:00 – 56:00]

- The focus should be on service; scriptural study is not intellectual study for its own sake.
- We study Kṛṣṇa consciousness philosophy not to intellectually scrutinise Kṛṣṇa but to intellectually submit to Him — to use our intelligence in His service.
- We don't place Kṛṣṇa on the altar of our intelligence, looking down to decide whether this is right or not right.
- Kṛṣṇa has given us various faculties, one of which is intelligence; if you have an intellectual tendency, use it in His service.
- Study removes misconceptions and increases conviction `56:00`.

<Callout type="idea" title="Takeaway">
Kṛṣṇa does not demand great intelligence before devotion; to those who serve Him with love — not grudgingly — He Himself gives the intelligence that leads to Him, so our intellect is meant to submit to Kṛṣṇa, not to sit in judgement over Him. [46:00 – 56:00]
</Callout>

### Bhagavad-gita 10.11 [56:00 – 61:22]

<!-- vedabase:10.11 -->

**Context.** Out of compassion, Kṛṣṇa, dwelling in the heart, destroys with the shining lamp of knowledge the darkness born of ignorance `56:00`.

**Key Terms Explained by the Teacher**

- _Blinding darkness_ — we cannot really think `56:00`.
- _Binding darkness_ — we don't know what is good for us, what is bad for us, or what is inside us `56:00 – 58:00`.

#### The flashlight in the dark [56:00]

- In darkness we try to get some source of light before we start moving around; darkness causes confusion, even paralysis — we can't do anything.
- We try to keep our outer world careful, but we don't think about our inner world, which is in blinding and binding darkness.

#### Not everything that comes from within is "me" [58:00]

- We don't know that Kṛṣṇa is in our heart, and we can't easily identify the emotions that arise.
- We assume, "Whatever comes from within is me — my emotion — I must act on it."
- But inside there is the soul, and along with it the mind; so not everything that comes out of me is me — it could be something masking itself and pretending to be me.

#### Kṛṣṇa ignites the light [58:00]

- Removing this darkness is not about exhaustive intellectual analysis; practise devotional service and Kṛṣṇa removes it.
- He ignites the light that makes the inner world bright and right.
- Then we can discern: material desires that promise pleasure are actually traitors that make us miserable — "I won't indulge them"; devotional desires are my friends — "I should nourish them." They nourish _bhakti_; love for Kṛṣṇa increases, and ultimately we return to Him.

#### Benevolence along with supremacy [60:00]

- Against the idea that devotees are not intelligent, the teacher names great _ācāryas_ such as Jīva Gosvāmī, Rāmānujācārya and Madhvācārya.
- In these four verses Kṛṣṇa demonstrates His supreme position, and along with it His benevolence: "If you just want to love Me — I care for you so much — I will personally intervene and give you the intelligence by which you will come back to Me."
- The call: don't worry, just follow Me; I will provide everything — just offer your love to Me.

<Callout type="idea" title="Takeaway">
Our inner world is in blinding and binding darkness, where every arising emotion seems to be "me"; not exhaustive analysis but devotional service lets Kṛṣṇa, from within, ignite the light that shows which desires are traitors and which are friends. [56:00 – 60:00]
</Callout>
